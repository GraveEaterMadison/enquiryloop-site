# EnquiryLoop — first customer outreach

## WhatsApp message (personalise before sending)
Hello! I'm building a simple admissions follow-up system for independent coaching centres. It helps organise student enquiries, track who needs a callback, and see how many enquiries turn into admissions.

I'm speaking with a few institute owners to understand how they currently manage enquiries. Would you be open to a 10-minute conversation? I'm not asking you to buy anything.

## In-person opening
"How do new student enquiries reach you today — phone calls, WhatsApp, walk-ins or forms? Where do you record them, and how do you make sure every interested student gets a follow-up?"

## Discovery questions
1. Roughly how many enquiries arrive in a normal month and during admission season?
2. Where are they recorded today — notebook, spreadsheet, WhatsApp or software?
3. Who is responsible for calling each enquiry back?
4. What happens when a counsellor is absent or busy?
5. How do you know which enquiries are still waiting for a response?
6. Do you track why interested students do not enrol?
7. What software have you tried? What did you dislike?
8. If one part of this process were fixed, which would matter most?
9. What would a useful solution be worth to the institute?
10. Would you pay for a small pilot if it solved that specific problem?

## Ground rules
- Do not send bulk unsolicited messages.
- Do not promise guaranteed admissions or revenue growth.
- Do not collect student personal data during discovery.
- Do not connect unofficial WhatsApp automation or ask for account passwords.
- Do not build features before a customer confirms the problem.
