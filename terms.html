Service

EnquiryLoop provides tools for organising education-business enquiries and follow-ups. Features and availability may change during early access.

Account responsibility

You are responsible for account security, workspace access and ensuring information you upload is accurate and lawfully collected. Do not upload sensitive personal data or information you are not authorised to manage.

Plans and payments

The free plan is intended to support up to 50 enquiries per workspace. Paid plans and online checkout are not yet active. Do not purchase a paid subscription until checkout, billing terms and cancellation procedures are published.

Availability

The service is in development. Do not rely on it as your only record system until backups and export procedures are operational.
