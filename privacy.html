EnquiryLoop Privacy Notice

Draft notice — review with a qualified professional before commercial launch.

EnquiryLoop helps education businesses organise enquiries. When configured, account information and workspace records are stored with the selected cloud database provider. Workspace access is restricted using authentication and database access policies.

Information entered

Users may enter student/customer names, contact details, course interests, follow-up dates and notes. Workspace owners must ensure they have the necessary authority and lawful basis to collect and use this information and provide required notices to students or guardians.

Security and retention

Do not enter sensitive personal information. Data retention, account deletion, subprocessors, international transfers, backups and incident response must be finalised before production launch.
